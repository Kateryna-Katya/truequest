Not your average puzzle

Look, I know what you're thinking — another match-3 game, right? But this one's actually different.

Hello Kitty Merch Match goes beyond traditional match-3 by adding a strong collection layer. Players don’t just complete levels — they gather items, decorate spaces, and interact with familiar characters. There's something weirdly satisfying about watching your little collection grow with each level
